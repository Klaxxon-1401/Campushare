# User Interview Guide

> **Week 1 deliverable** · §15.1 support
> **Participants:** 6–10 students + 1–2 campus administrators · 25–35 min each
> **Method:** Semi-structured, recorded (with consent). One interviewer + one notetaker.
> **Goal:** Qualitatively validate personas, uncover pain points survey cannot reach, and probe trust/dispute expectations.

---

## Participant Profile Matrix (target)

| Role | Count | Source | Recruitment method |
|------|-------|--------|-------------------|
| Prospective borrower | 3–4 | General student body | Flyer + student associations |
| Prospective lender | 3–4 | Students who own expensive occasional-use items (e.g. calculators, suits) | Faculty referral, social media |
| Campus admin / student affairs | 1–2 | Student affairs office | Direct outreach via formal email |

Aim for mix across year of study and at least one international student.

---

## Pre-Session

- Confirm informed verbal consent + audio recording permission.
- Reminder: "There are no right or wrong answers — you're helping us understand real student needs."

---

## Interview Script

### Opening & Background (5 min)

1. Tell me a bit about yourself — year, study area, roughly what a typical week looks like.
2. On a scale of 1–5, how price-conscious are you about day-to-day spending?

### Demand & Current Behavior (8 min)

3. Walk me through the last time you needed something for class or an event that you didn't own. What did you end up doing?
4. *[Probe if survey hinted at borrow-from-friend answers]*
   - How did that work out? Any awkwardness or unresolved issue?
5. Has anyone ever asked you to borrow something you own — how did you handle it?

### Rental Concept Reaction (7 min)

6. We're designing a campus-only app where verified students can rent items like lab coats, blazers, calculators, etc. Hear me out… *(briefly describe concept + 4 trust pillars)*.
   - First reaction — what's your gut feeling?
   - What's the first thing that would worry you?
   - What would need to be true for you to actually use it?

### Trust & Disputes Deep-Dive (8 min)

7. Imagine you rented a blazer and it came back with a stain. Walk me through what you'd want to happen — step by step.
8. What would you want to see as proof of the item's condition before you handed it over? *[leads to condition-photo concept]*
9. If a deposit of ₹30 was held by the app and returned in 48h, would that feel safe or risky? What amount would make you uncomfortable?
10. How important is it that the other person is a verified student vs. an anonymous stranger?

### Listing & Ease of Use (5 min)

11. How would you feel about taking photos and filling in a quick form to list an item? What would make that feel worth your time?
12. What's your expectation on how long the book-and-pay process should take?

### Closing (2 min)

13. What's the one thing a student rental app MUST get right for you to recommend it to friends?
14. Anything I didn't ask that you think is important?
15. Can we contact you for a quick follow-up if needed? *(Y/N)*

## Post-Interview

- Debrief between interviewer + notetaker: capture 3–5 theme tags immediately.
- Tag observations against persona hypotheses (see `../personas/validation-plan.md`): confirm, refute, refine.
- Note verbatim quotes that illustrate strong emotional responses — mark for PRD/WiP doc use.
