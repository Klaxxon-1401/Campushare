# Distribution & Recruitment Plan

> **Week 1 deliverable** · supporting Survey 1 + interviews
> **Target:** 200 survey responses + 6–10 interviews + 1–2 admin interviews

---

## Survey Distribution

| Channel | Owner | When | Target count |
|---------|-------|------|-------------|
| Campus-wide email blast (students@university.edu) | Team lead | Mon Week 1 | 50–80 |
| Social media posts (student Facebook/WhatsApp groups, X, Instagram stories) | MarComm student | Mon–Fri Week 1 | 40–60 |
| QR posters in library, student union, cafeterias, lab buildings | MarComm student | Mon Week 1 (print + post) | 40–50 |
| Faculty-forwarded in class announcements (3–5 sympathetic lecturers) | Team lead (ask) | Week 1 | 30–40 |
| Student association newsletters | MarComm student | Mon + Wed | 20–30 |

**Quota monitor:** Daily check of response count. If < 50% by Wednesday, add second social media push + hallway intercepts (team members approach students directly).

---

## Interview Recruitment

**Borrower-profile (3–4):**
- Send targeted email to 2nd/3rd-year students who replied Yes to Survey B1 and gave contact.
- Supplement with direct outreach to known student organization members.
- Offer ₹15 coffee voucher for their time.

**Lender-profile (3–4):**
- Post in campus buy-sell/trade groups asking "Do you own [calculator/suit/camera] you rarely use?"
- Direct outreach via faculty referrals.

**Campus admin (1–2):**
- Formal email to Student Affairs office referencing project and requesting 20–30 min meeting. Frame as consultation, not sales pitch.

---

## Ethics & Privacy

- Survey anonymous; no identifying fields stored.
- Interviews recorded only with explicit verbal consent; recordings transcribed and deleted after Week 2.
- All data anonymized before analysis; raw recordings stored on encrypted, team-only drive with 3-month retention.
